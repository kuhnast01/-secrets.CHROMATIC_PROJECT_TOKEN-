# Incident Response & Escalation

## Incident Detection
- Monitor logs, alerts, and error reports for signs of incidents (downtime, data loss, security breach).
- Use automated monitoring tools (Sentry, Datadog, custom health checks).

## Initial Response
- Triage incident severity (critical, major, minor).
- Assign incident lead and notify relevant team members.
- Communicate status to stakeholders (internal and, if needed, external).

## Containment & Mitigation
- Contain the impact (e.g., disable affected services, revoke credentials, block access).
- Apply hotfixes or roll back to last known good state if needed.

## Investigation & Resolution
- Gather logs, metrics, and evidence.
- Identify root cause and contributing factors.
- Implement fixes and verify resolution.

## Postmortem & Follow-Up
- Document incident timeline, impact, and actions taken.
- Hold a post-incident review to identify lessons learned.
- Update runbooks, documentation, and processes to prevent recurrence.

## Escalation
- Escalate to senior engineers, security, or management as needed.
- Maintain an up-to-date escalation contact list.
